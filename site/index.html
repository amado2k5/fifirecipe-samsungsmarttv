Cook together, on the big screen

Made for the remote

Every screen is built for the Samsung remote: arrows move a bright focus ring, Enter opens, Return goes back — and on the home screen Return takes you straight back to Smart Hub.

Every recipe, localized

Browse chapters, search with the on-screen keyboard, and read ingredients and numbered steps from across the room — in 26 languages with full right-to-left layouts.

Kids in the kitchen

A bright, illustrated Kids mode with get-ready checklists, allergen banners, grown-up alerts, big step cards and a celebration when the dish is done.

Watch and learn

Recipe videos play full screen through privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds. Play/Pause and Stop on the remote work as you'd expect.
